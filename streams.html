Confused about which stream to pick after Class 10? Explore each option and see where it can take you.

💡 How to Use This Page

After Class 10, your stream choice shapes which careers are easier to enter. Read each card below, look at the careers it leads to, and click "Explore Careers →" to see matching careers on the main Career Explorer page. Remember: no stream is "better" than another — it depends on what you enjoy and what you're good at.
